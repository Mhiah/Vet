ANSWER: Probably yes, but only if the retailer has enough margin to absorb BNPL fees and sells products where higher conversion or larger baskets can offset those costs. BNPL is most likely to help for higher-ticket discretionary items and mobile-heavy traffic; it is less likely to be worth it for low-margin, low-AOV, or fraud-prone categories.
